I volunteer as an instructor at **FAC**, a non-profit in Mexico City, where I have taught since July 2025. Classes run **every second Saturday**, in person, in **English and Spanish**, with 10–30 students in each session.

## Cybersecurity (Saturday mornings)

A practical cybersecurity class: how attacks happen and how to defend against them, grounded in incidents I have handled in industry.

## Self-hosting (Saturday afternoons)

A hands-on class on running your own services on your own hardware instead of renting them.

## Mentoring

Outside the classroom I mentor junior DevSecOps engineers in Mexico City, helping them build practical skills in cloud, automation and security.
